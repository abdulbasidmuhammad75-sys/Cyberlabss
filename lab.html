📍 Device GPS

When you press GET MY LOCATION, your browser asks for location permission. If you allow it, the browser can provide coordinates from the device's available location systems.

🌐 IP Geolocation

An IP address can provide approximate network geography such as country, region or city. It should not be treated as an exact physical GPS position.
